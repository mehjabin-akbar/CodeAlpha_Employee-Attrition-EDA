\# Employee Attrition — Exploratory Data Analysis



\## Project Overview



This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition \& Performance dataset.



The analysis focuses on understanding employee characteristics, identifying patterns associated with employee attrition, detecting potential data-quality issues and examining relationships between important variables.



\## Objectives



\- Understand the structure and characteristics of the dataset.

\- Explore employee demographics and job-related factors.

\- Analyze patterns associated with employee attrition.

\- Examine relationships between important variables.

\- Detect missing values, duplicate records and potential outliers.

\- Test selected hypotheses using statistical analysis.

\- Identify potential areas for further analysis.



\## Dataset



The dataset contains employee-related information including:



\- Age

\- Department

\- Job Role

\- Monthly Income

\- Job Satisfaction

\- Overtime

\- Years at Company

\- Total Working Years

\- Attrition

\- And other employee and job-related variables.



\## Analysis Performed



The following EDA techniques were applied:



1\. Dataset structure and data types

2\. Missing-value analysis

3\. Duplicate-record analysis

4\. Unique-value analysis

5\. Descriptive statistics

6\. Univariate analysis

7\. Bivariate analysis

8\. Attrition-rate analysis

9\. Correlation analysis

10\. Outlier detection using the IQR method

11\. Chi-Square hypothesis testing

12\. Key findings and data-quality assessment



\## Key Questions



The analysis investigates questions such as:



\- What is the overall employee attrition rate?

\- Is attrition associated with overtime?

\- How does attrition vary across job roles?

\- Does attrition differ across age groups?

\- Does monthly income differ between employees who stayed and left?

\- Is job satisfaction associated with attrition?

\- Does years at the company show different attrition patterns?

\- Is there a relationship between age and monthly income?

\- Are there potential outliers or data-quality issues?
